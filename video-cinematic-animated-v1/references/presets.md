# Presets

Starting Shot Graphs. Copy the structure, adjust. Full working example: `assets/examples/product-ad.plan.json`.

## product-ad
Aspect 9:16 or 16:9. Total 12 to 15 s. Identity: product (locked, ref photo required).
1. Hook (2): extreme close-up macro detail, crash zoom or rack focus.
2. Hero (4): orbit or static, practical rim light. Weight cue: contact shadow, reflection tracking.
3. Benefit (4): medium, tracking or push-in, product in use.
4. Payoff (3): pull-back reveal or crane up, clean negative space for CTA.
Audio: music bed plus one foley hit per shot. Text only if requested.
Chain: shots 1 to 2 from product keyframe; re-anchor to ref at shot 3.

## social-reel
Aspect 9:16. Total 8 to 15 s. Hook in first 1.5 s.
1. Hook (1.5 to 2.5): whip pan, fpv, or crash zoom.
2. Develop (3 to 5): tracking or handheld, subject in motion.
3. Turn (2 to 4): dolly zoom or bullet time at the key beat.
4. Payoff (2 to 3): pull-back reveal, last frame resembles frame 1 for looping.
Max 4 shots. Draft everything first.

## talking-head
Aspect 9:16 or 16:9. Per clip 5 to 8 s (lip-sync drifts when longer).
Identity: character with face ref (required). Use a talking-capable model class.
Alternate medium close-up (static or slow dolly in) and close-up. Micro-expression weight cues. Keep eyeline constant.
Audio: speech per sentence group, not the whole monologue.
Chain only 2 clips then re-anchor to the face ref.
